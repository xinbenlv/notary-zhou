# Repository instructions

This file defines the editorial language policy for Notary Zhou.

## Article language

- Publish articles, guides, and editorial FAQs only in Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`). This includes article titles, summaries/descriptions, and body text.
- Do not create, translate, or publish English versions of articles or restore retired English article routes.
- Keep the website interface bilingual (Chinese and English), including navigation, buttons, directory headings, booking controls, and footers. The article language policy does not remove English service pages or interface text.
- Switching the interface language must keep the reader on the same article and leave its title, summary, and body in `zh-CN`.
