**When names do not match, start with the records that explain the difference, then establish whether an affidavit is needed.** A same person affidavit, also called a one and the same person affidavit, is your statement about the relationship between names or records. In a California jurat, the notary identifies the person appearing and administers the oath; the notary [does not certify that every name in the statement belongs to that person](#ref-jurat-notice).

That distinction matters to readers worldwide, but the notarial steps here apply to California. A recipient in mainland China, Taiwan or elsewhere must confirm its own evidence requirements. One institution's form is not a universal standard.

## Is this a spelling difference, legal name change or records error?

Use the following as a way to organize the problem. It is not wording for an affidavit.

| Situation | Evidence to look for first | Question for the recipient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese characters, romanization or name order differs | The original documents and issuer details | Is a translation explanation enough, or is a personal statement required? |
| A legal name change occurred | The court order or other relevant official record | Which certified copies and old/new IDs are required? |
| One document contains a clerical error | The original application or correct records | Must the issuer correct it, or is supplementary evidence allowed? |
| An everyday English name differs from the legal name | Documents actually using that name | What evidence will establish an acceptable connection? |

California Courts explains that a judge's name-change order states the new legal name and is used to update identity documents. For clerical errors on identity documents, it directs readers to [contact the issuing agency](#ref-ca-name). If a name-change order already exists or a document plainly contains an error, do not automatically start by writing another statement.

Taiwan's household registration system separately offers a [name-change record certificate](#ref-taiwan-name), obtainable by the person concerned or an authorized representative under its rules. It documents a change in that records system, which is different from a U.S. notary witnessing a private affidavit. An overseas recipient must still confirm whether that certificate meets its needs.

## Match each name to its evidence

![Concept illustration: a passport, identity card and certificate index the source of each name instead of assuming that the names already identify one person](/images/articles/same-person-affidavit/03-name-table.jpg)

A useful working sheet lists each name as it appears, the document containing it, the issuing authority and any record explaining the change. This is an organizational aid; it does not mean every field belongs in the final affidavit.

**Keep the discrepancy visible.** If a passport and bank record use different spellings, the recipient needs to see the difference and its source. Rewriting every name into one preferred form hides the issue you are trying to explain. Ask which identification numbers must be supplied and whether unrelated information may be redacted; avoid circulating full identity documents without a purpose.

If a statement is required, obtain its format, attachment list and signing instructions from the recipient. The Chinese Consulate General in San Francisco similarly directs applicants to [prepare statements according to the receiving institution's requirements](#ref-sf-text). A California notary cannot decide which legal relationship to assert: the Secretary of State identifies drafting or selecting legal documents and giving related legal advice as [work a notary should decline and refer to an attorney](#ref-ca-legal).

An affidavit does not itself update an official record. A court order, an issuer's correction and a private statement do different work. Confirm which combination the institution handling your transaction requires.

## If the affidavit needs a California jurat

A sworn affidavit commonly involves a jurat. California requires the signer to [appear personally, sign in the notary's presence and take an oath or affirmation](#ref-ca-jurat). Bring the completed document and required attachments, with the signature left for the appointment.

There is a specific exception to the shorthand “the notary does not check contents.” If a document purporting to identify the affiant includes both a birth date or age and a photograph or fingerprint, Government Code section 8230 requires verification of the stated age or birth date. [It specifies a certified birth certificate or DMV-issued identification card or driver's license, with a separate provision for certain immigration-form purposes](#ref-ca-8230). Tell the notary about such a format before the appointment so the required evidence can be confirmed.

Also distinguish attachments from certified copies. **Notarizing the affidavit signature does not officially certify every photocopy attached to it.** If the recipient requires a certified copy, establish which authority can issue that particular copy. Simply stapling it to a sworn statement does not turn it into an issuer-certified record.

## Mainland China: an Apostille authenticates the official signature

If a recipient requires a U.S. document to follow the Convention route, identify the appropriate Apostille authority from the document's origin. The Chinese consulate's notice states that, from November 7, 2023, [covered U.S. public documents can be used in mainland China with a U.S. Apostille instead of consular legalization](#ref-china-apostille).

An Apostille [does not verify the contents](#ref-ca-apostille). The Secretary of State has therefore not investigated whether the listed names belong to the same person. The consular notice also warns that an Apostille does not guarantee recipient acceptance; ask about content, format, age and translation before proceeding.

For a California Apostille, [the notarization must be in English, while the document body may use another language](#ref-ca-language). Any required translation or translator's certification is a separate issue. See the [Apostille guide](/en/articles/apostille-for-china/), or the [power of attorney guide](/en/articles/china-power-of-attorney/) if the mismatch affects authority to act on someone else's behalf.

For Taiwan or another destination, check the relevant authentication rules separately. Changing the destination name on the cover sheet is not a substitute for that check.

## Before booking

1. Get the recipient's requirements for statements, official records, attachments and translations.
2. Locate existing name-change records; contact the issuer about errors when appropriate.
3. Have the recipient or a lawyer confirm the document and notarial type. Do not treat an online example as a legal form.
4. Tell the local notary about the signing language, identification and any photograph or fingerprint requirements.
