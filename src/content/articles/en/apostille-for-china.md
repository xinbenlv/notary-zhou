**The right apostille office depends on who issued the document.** A state birth certificate, a personally signed statement and a federal record do not all follow a California notary route. Start with the document's issuer and the signature being authenticated.[Authority directory](#ref-us-authorities)

Since **November 7, 2023**, covered U.S. public documents for use in mainland China follow the [U.S. apostille route instead of the former consular legalization process](#ref-china-notice). An apostille still does not guarantee acceptance. Confirm what the receiving institution needs before paying for the paperwork.

## Match the document to its authority

| Document | Start here | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State or county birth, marriage or other government record | Obtain a certified copy from the record custodian | Apply to the designated authority for that state or jurisdiction |
| Personal affidavit, power of attorney or similar statement | Confirm the wording and required notarial act with the recipient | Follow the rules where that act occurs, then use the corresponding authority |
| U.S. federal agency record | Check that agency's document instructions | Usually the State Department's Office of Authentications; federal court records have designated court officials |
| Document issued outside the United States | Check the issuing jurisdiction's rules | Use its designated authority, rather than assuming a U.S. process applies |

These distinctions follow the [U.S. authority directory](#ref-us-authorities) and the [Convention's authority rules](#ref-hcch-authority). Notarial acts and official signature certificates on private documents can fall within the Convention. That [does not require every private document to be notarized](#ref-hcch-scope).

Living in California does not turn a New York birth certificate into a California record. A statement to be signed before a California notary presents a different question: the official act being authenticated occurs there.

For document-specific steps, see [birth certificate apostilles](/en/articles/birth-certificate-apostille/) and [marriage certificate apostilles](/en/articles/marriage-certificate-apostille/).

## What an apostille establishes

It authenticates the relevant [signature, official capacity and seal](#ref-hcch-effect), rather than [validating the document's contents](#ref-ca-purpose). It does not decide whether a power of attorney gives enough authority, a translation meets the recipient's standards or an application should be approved.

Keep the recipient's written instructions alongside the document. Before applying, ask:

- Which record and version are needed, and how recently must it have been issued?
- How many copies are needed, and in what format?
- Is a translation required, who may prepare it, and does it need separate certification?
- Do the names, dates and proposed use match the recipient's requirements?

The Chinese consulate specifically advises checking [format, content, timing and translation requirements](#ref-china-acceptance).

## Changing the destination changes the checks

The U.S.–mainland China route is not a universal procedure for every country or document. The Convention has [document exclusions](#ref-hcch-scope), and its operation can depend on [relations between the particular contracting parties](#ref-hcch-relations).

For another origin or destination, consult the [HCCH status table and declarations](#ref-hcch-status), then the issuing authority and recipient. Where the Convention does not apply between the jurisdictions, an apostille cannot be assumed to replace legalization. The consulate's notice separately addresses Hong Kong and Macao; requirements for Taiwan and other destinations should not be inferred from its mainland China instructions.

## California as an example

For a document California can process, use the [Secretary of State's current application instructions](#ref-ca-request). A mail request generally includes the eligible document, a cover sheet identifying the destination country, payment and a return envelope. An ordinary photocopy is not accepted in place of the required notarized or certified document.

As checked on September 15, 2026, the fee is **$20 per apostille**. In-person requests add **$6 for each different public official's signature**; that extra fee does not apply by mail. Copy, translation and delivery charges are separate.[Fee schedule](#ref-ca-fees)

Other jurisdictions have their own fees. Check current addresses, payment methods and processing updates before arranging an urgent trip; courier transit time is not government processing time.

## Identify what actually needs notarization

California notaries [cannot certify copies of birth, death or marriage records](#ref-ca-vital-records). Obtain those copies from the authorized record custodian. A personal statement is a separate document: have the recipient or an appropriate professional settle its wording and required notarial form first.
