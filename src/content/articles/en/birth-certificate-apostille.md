**For a state or county birth certificate, obtain a suitable certified copy from the birth jurisdiction, then apply to that jurisdiction's apostille authority.** Living in California does not make California the correct office for another state's record.[Record source](#ref-usa-birth) · [Authority directory](#ref-us-authorities)

This guide covers U.S. birth records for use abroad. A U.S. Consular Report of Birth Abroad (CRBA) follows a separate route. Birth records issued by China or another country also do not follow the U.S. state process.

## 1. Identify the exact record the recipient needs

Ask the receiving institution to confirm the record name, whether parent information must appear, acceptable issue dates, number of copies and translation requirements. If it asks for a birth notarial certificate issued by a Chinese notarial office, clarify whether a U.S. government record is acceptable before treating those as interchangeable.

For a U.S. birth, USAGov directs you to the [birth state or territory's vital records office](#ref-usa-birth). That office provides ordering methods, fees and identification requirements. Tell it the destination country and ask which version is suitable for an apostille. A scan or ordinary photocopy is not already a certified copy.

## 2. Check the official signature

**California example:** the Secretary of State lists signatures of county clerks and deputies, county recorders and deputies, and the State Registrar. A local record signed by a Health Officer or County Registrar [may need county-clerk certification or replacement with a certified copy from the County Recorder or State Registrar](#ref-ca-signatures).

“May need” does not mean every record requires an extra visit. Check the signature, printed name and title at the bottom, then confirm eligibility with the apostille office. “Issued by the county” alone is not enough detail.

Other states have their own rules. California's list does not settle the requirements for New York, Texas or another jurisdiction.

## 3. Apply, then submit the required package

Follow the issuing state's instructions for the eligible record, application, payment and return delivery. For California, use the [official application page](#ref-ca-request). The [apostille overview](/en/articles/apostille-for-china/) explains the wider routing distinctions and California fee example.

Since November 7, 2023, covered U.S. public documents for mainland China [no longer follow the former consular legalization route](#ref-china-route). An apostille does not guarantee acceptance: the recipient's format, content, timing and translation requirements still matter.[Acceptance boundary](#ref-china-acceptance)

For a child's household registration, school enrollment or another administrative procedure, obtain the responsible office's written checklist. This guide addresses document authentication; it does not determine nationality or eligibility for those procedures.

## Three distinctions that prevent the wrong application

**Should a notary certify the birth certificate?** California notaries [cannot certify copies of birth records](#ref-ca-notary). An application form may separately require notarization of the applicant's statement. That does not mean adding a notary stamp to the birth certificate.

**Does a CRBA go to a state office?** A CRBA is a U.S. consular birth report. The [HCCH directory](#ref-us-authorities) assigns apostilles for consular birth records to the State Department's Bureau of Consular Affairs, Passport Services, Vital Records Section. Do not treat it as a state birth certificate.

**Can a translation replace the record?** Ask what the recipient needs and whether the record and translation have separate certification requirements. A translation, a government-certified copy and an apostille serve different purposes; naming the exact item is more useful than requesting “birth certificate notarization.”
