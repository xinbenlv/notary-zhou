**Start with the place and purpose of use before ordering a “single status certificate.”** A U.S. citizen marrying in Taiwan, a Taiwan resident preparing to marry abroad, and someone signing a document in California for mainland China need different checklists. A government record, your own statement and a notarization perform different jobs.

This guide explains those distinctions and the California signing process. It is not a universal affidavit form or a determination that you are legally eligible to marry.

## What document is the recipient asking for?

| Requested evidence | Who provides it | What to clarify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marriage record or search result | The relevant records authority | Geographic coverage, years and record types |
| Single status affidavit | You make the statement; a notary administers the required formalities | Whether that statement and format are accepted |
| Evidence that a previous marriage ended | The relevant court or records authority | Required divorce or death records, authentication and translation |

The U.S. Department of State says [the U.S. government cannot attest to your marital status, although a destination may accept your written statement of eligibility to marry](#ref-state-marriage). That does not mean local marriage records do not exist. It also does not mean every destination will accept a document you draft yourself. The same guidance directs travelers to confirm local requirements, including proof of the end of previous marriages, translations and authentication.

A search finding no record within its specified scope is not proof that no marriage exists anywhere in the world. Ask the recipient whether it wants a records search or permits an affidavit in place of other evidence.

## Marrying in Taiwan: confirm your category and where the document is made

Taiwan's Department of Household Registration has separate instructions for [foreign-national spouses, marriages already effective abroad, and marriages involving people from mainland China](#ref-taiwan-marriage). Chinese-language readers do not all follow the same route.

For a foreign-national spouse, the checklist includes a declaration adopting a Chinese name and evidence of marital status authenticated by the relevant overseas mission. It states that the marital-status evidence is valid for **six months from its original issuance**. Overseas documents and Chinese translations have additional authentication requirements. [These are Taiwan registration requirements](#ref-taiwan-marriage), not a worldwide expiry rule for affidavits.

Before a U.S. citizen signs anything for a Taiwan marriage registration, give the household registration office your nationality, proposed signing location and document name. Confirm the authentication process with the responsible mission as well. **Do not treat the U.S.-to-mainland-China Apostille route as a complete Taiwan checklist.** If you are registering a marriage already effective abroad, use the corresponding instructions rather than automatically preparing premarital documents again.

If you need evidence held within Taiwan's household registration system, a separate [marriage-record certificate application](#ref-taiwan-record) is available to the person concerned or an authorized representative under its rules. That is a different document from an affidavit signed in the United States. For use abroad, ask the destination whether it accepts the certificate and what authentication or translation is needed.

## Preparing a U.S. document for mainland China

Tell the actual marriage registry or other recipient your nationality, residence and purpose. Ask it to identify the required evidence. Being in the United States does not by itself establish that U.S. notarization is necessary, and a marriage-registration checklist should not automatically be reused for property or household-registration matters.

For eligible Chinese citizens, the Chinese Consulate General in San Francisco offers [notarization of a declaration of no spouse](#ref-sf-single). Its eligibility rules concern Chinese citizens residing in its consular district. Previously divorced or widowed applicants must provide the listed supporting documents. The consulate describes a general six-month validity period and requires personal signing. **Those are that consulate's service rules, not the rules for every U.S. affidavit.**

If the mainland recipient accepts your U.S. document and it falls within the Apostille Convention, the consulate's notice states that, from November 7, 2023, [covered U.S. public documents can be used in mainland China after a U.S. Apostille, without U.S. and Chinese consular legalization](#ref-china-apostille). An Apostille does not guarantee acceptance of the document's contents; confirm format, age and translation requirements first.

See the [Apostille guide for U.S. documents used in mainland China](/en/articles/apostille-for-china/) for the document-origin distinction.

## What the California notary does

![Concept illustration: a personal statement and identification on one side, supporting records on the other; neither replaces the other](/images/articles/single-status-affidavit/02-who-vouches.jpg)

When the required document is a sworn affidavit, its jurat requires you to [appear personally, sign in the notary's presence, and take an oath or affirmation](#ref-ca-jurat). California's certificate identifies the signer; it [does not certify the truth, accuracy or validity of the statement](#ref-ca-jurat-notice). Notarization is not a government investigation of your marriage history.

Before arranging the appointment, clarify these points with the recipient:

1. Does it require a statement of never having married, or of currently having no spouse? Must previous marriages and their termination be described?
2. Which supporting records, original copies and languages are required, and how soon must the signed document be submitted?
3. Which notarial and authentication procedures are required, and is there a prescribed document?
4. If the wording needs legal judgment, have a lawyer familiar with the destination review it. [California notaries may not draft or select legal documents for customers](#ref-ca-legal).

Leave the signature blank for a jurat appointment. For a California Apostille, [the notarization must be in English, but the rest of the document may be in another language](#ref-ca-language). A bilingual document does not automatically meet the recipient's translation requirements.

## If the names do not match

A **same person affidavit** addresses the relationship between names or records; it does not establish marital status. If names differ, consult the [guide to same person affidavits and official name-change records](/en/articles/same-person-affidavit/) and ask whether the recipient requires separate evidence.

Outside California, confirm signing and authentication requirements with the relevant local authority.
