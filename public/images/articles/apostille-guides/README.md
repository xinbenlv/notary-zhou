# Apostille guide covers

Original Chinese covers for the three apostille guides. Source SVGs and derived progressive JPEGs share a basename; article frontmatter uses the JPEGs for broad social-card support. The diagram is a preparation sequence, not a legal certificate or promise of acceptance. Articles and their covers follow the Chinese-only editorial policy.

```
apostille-guides/
├── apostille-for-china-zh.{svg,jpg}
├── birth-certificate-apostille-zh.{svg,jpg}
├── marriage-certificate-apostille-zh.{svg,jpg}
├── manifest.json
└── README.md
```

All covers are 1200 × 630, with local-font Chinese text. SVG sources contain accessible names and descriptions. The JPEGs were rasterized from those sources with Sharp. No external fonts, logos, personal records or generated legal seals are used. English-specific variants have been removed; retain the Chinese SVGs and JPEGs unchanged.

The guides' sections are short procedural explanations. Their tables and numbered instructions carry the necessary distinctions, so additional section illustrations would repeat the prose. Each cover shows the document-routing sequence. See `manifest.json` for titles and purpose.
