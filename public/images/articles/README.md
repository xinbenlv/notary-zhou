# Article artwork

Public FAQ, guides and articles are Chinese only. Preserve Chinese covers and shared text-free illustrations at their existing URLs. Do not add English-only article covers or translation variants. Legacy article folders retain their artwork; apostille covers live in `apostille-guides/`, and identity covers use `identity-*-zh.svg`.

Keep descriptive Chinese alt text in article frontmatter or Markdown. Keep source provenance with the related article or asset README. Removing an article does not authorize deleting an illustration that a retained Chinese page uses.

```text
articles/
├── <legacy-article>/
├── apostille-guides/    Chinese SVG/JPEG cover pairs and manifest
└── identity-*-zh.svg    Chinese identity-topic covers
```
